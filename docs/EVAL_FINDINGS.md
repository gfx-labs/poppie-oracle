# Poppie on Arbitrum with the v1 contracts: evaluation findings

*Evaluation of `docs/legacy/Design.md` against the audited v1 code. Branch `v2`; `src/` is identical to tag `v1-bsc` (`git diff v1-bsc HEAD -- src` is empty). No v1 source was modified. Live networks were only read (`eth_call`, logs, public APIs) or forked locally; nothing was broadcast.*

## Summary

- **Splits and rebases can be handled without modifying the v1 contracts.** The v1 `PoppieEulerOracle` and `PoppieEulerAdapter` work as they are, provided rebasing tokens are held through a non-rebasing ERC-4626 wrapper. Issuer wrappers exist for xStocks and Dinari. The wrapper share becomes the oracle base.
- **Dividends the protocol must claim, or that arrive as stablecoins, need one small new contract.** Neither an EVK escrow vault nor an unmodified OpenZeppelin `ERC4626` can recover tokens sent to it. A minimal dividend-capture wrapper (about 100 lines) lets the protocol be the holder of record, claim and sweep dividends, and count them as revenue.
- **The main operational risk is a wrong price push at a split.** The oracle cannot tell a split from a market move. A wrong 2-for-1 push passes the 5000 bps breaker used in the dev scripts, and moves above +100% need `adminSetPrice`. This is handled off-chain by the corporate-action engine.
- **The wrapper share must be the oracle base; never route through to the rebasing token.** Through the stock EulerRouter, pricing the wrapper share directly keeps collateral value continuous when a multiplier activates. Resolving the wrapper to the rebasing token instead (the pattern xStocks' docs describe: `convertToAssets` × token price) overvalues collateral 2× at a 2:1 split until the keeper pushes, and a 3:1 split cannot be fixed by the keeper at all (PATH-2).
- **xStocks contracts fit; the market doesn't exist yet (XS-1 to XS-6).** Fork tests on the real AAPLx token and V2 wrapper confirm the pricing path, including a split. But over 99% of Arbitrum supply of the major names is Backed inventory, the V2 wrappers are unused and there are no Uniswap v3 USDC pools, so liquidators have no exit today. On-chain, the only exit is Backed's own inventory through its allowlisted `AtomicSwap` RFQ, which CoW solvers also use. Backed (2026-11-04) quotes up to $1M per trade on majors in market hours, $10k per trade on long-tail names overnight, and nothing executable at weekends. It will not liquidate itself; solvers on CoW can. An atomic route needs an onboarded party: Poppie won't onboard and runs no liquidator at launch, but Enso is already allowlisted, so third-party liquidators can sell atomically through it (XS-7, D9). Multiplier notice is normally about 2 hours but can be 15 minutes, with no on-chain minimum; Backed publishes pending changes, with a reason, through an API (XS-9). Corporate-action payouts do not reach wrapper holders: holders must unwrap first (XS-8). On-chain history (XS-11 to XS-14) shows spin-offs so far were folded into the multiplier, so the wrapper followed them with no action. No merger or delisting with outside holders has completed yet; Kenvue (KVUEx) is the first pending case. On 2026-06-29 HONx's multiplier was halved for 8 h 25 min with about 5 minutes' notice, then reset 2.44% below its prior value (XS-13).
- **Robinhood Stock Tokens fit with no wrapper and no new contract (RH-1 to RH-11).** They are ERC-8056 tokens: balances never change, and a multiplier (`uiMultiplier()`) carries splits and reinvested dividends. The escrow holds the token and the token is the oracle base. Fork tests on the real AAPL token on Robinhood Chain confirm a 2:1 split is continuous through `EulerRouter` and the v1 adapter. The risks are issuer powers: two Robinhood EOAs can burn tokens from any holder, including an escrow (RH-5, accepted as issuer risk), and the multiplier key is unbounded (RH-4). On 2026-11-04 SOXX's multiplier was set to 3.0 for three hours and then reverted, with no matching corporate action published (RH-6). The tokens are only on Robinhood Chain today (RH-11).
- **Reality rTokens (Bitget) are live on Arbitrum and fit the direct route today, but the market and the issuer's record are the problem (RE-1 to RE-12).** Since 2026-10-17 they are ERC-8056 tokens with static balances, and a real rSOXX 3:1 split is continuous through `EulerRouter` and the v1 adapter on a fork. Before that date they rebased. The switch-over changed every holder's balance on 18 tokens in one beacon upgrade, which a raw-token EVK escrow could not have survived (RE-3). Splits come through an on-chain registry with about 3 days' notice, but the Safe can execute them early (RE-2). The record shows a split executed a week early and reversed, a reverse split executed twice (rHON, the day of the HONx incident), and splits left unexecuted past their effective time (RE-4). The 2026-11-05 SOXX split was applied at 08:30 UTC by Reality, 14:00 by xStocks and 14:30 by Robinhood (RE-5). Dividends, cash in USDT0 and stock in rTokens, belong to the depositor. Merkl traces them through supported protocols, and Reality sends them to the depositor's wallet. Poppie's escrows aren't supported, so by default depositors' dividends are withheld by Reality until Merkl support is added. They are neither stuck in the escrow nor Poppie revenue, and no wrapper is needed (RE-9). About 97–100% of each major name sits in two or three EOAs, and the only DEX liquidity is four ~$190k Uniswap pools. Atomic USDC redemption exists, but only for wallets Reality registers (RE-10). All contracts are unverified.
- **Scheduled issuer pauses need no new contract (PAUSE-1).** They are handled off-chain: the app and SDK block deposits and withdrawals during known windows, and the issuer's own pause enforces on-chain. An EVK hook is a deferred option.
- **v2 extends the BSC keeper, and a prototype now runs on an Arbitrum fork (KEEP-2 to KEEP-20).** The BSC keeper drops assets that would fail the on-chain guards (A1), retries a reverted batch, and has a halt latch and an alert-only Alpha Vantage check. It applies Ondo's multiplier with no corporate-action check. The prototype adds the blocking gates (D11), per-name isolation, a sequencer gate and chunking, and passes its split, rogue-multiplier, fee, pause, outage and scale scenarios against real xStocks contracts. What it found:
  - Data Streams covers only 71 xStocks names on Arbitrum (KEEP-4).
  - The multiplier gate needs keeper state (KEEP-7) and a corporate-action calendar feed (KEEP-14).
  - Backed's API is too slow to call for every name on every run; the keeper now calls it only on change (KEEP-6).
  - A real dividend activated overnight, and matches the calendar only net of withholding (KEEP-19).
  - The BSC keeper's Alpha Vantage check may compare nothing (KEEP-3), and its anvil suite fails on the base commit (KEEP-2).
- **Liquidations are left to third parties; thin liquidity is capped, not solved (D9).** Poppie will not onboard with any issuer and runs no liquidator at launch. Third parties can reach Backed's inventory without onboarding: atomically through Enso (allowlisted on Backed's `AtomicSwap`; 0x routes through it) or asynchronously through CoW (XS-7). None is committed, so exposure is limited by caps sized to exit depth per session, LTV buffers, liquidation discounts, concentration limits and the loss reserve.
- **Listability:** Ondo's docs do not list Arbitrum as a supported chain. Most of the Arbitrum set may need to come from xStocks and Dinari.

Build health: the default (audited) profile is unchanged. `forge build` passes (adapter 3,737 B runtime, oracle 7,385 B), `forge test` passes 121/121, and slither reports only `block.timestamp` comparisons.

Using captured dividends in borrow parameters: EVK has no per-collateral rate, so a dividend cannot lower a specific borrower's rate on-chain without more contracts. Off-chain uses:
- set LTV buffers above the largest expected dividend, including specials, because the wrapper share drops by the dividend on the ex-date;
- fund the loss reserve;

### Corporate-action pricing risk

- The breaker check is a strict `>` against the old price (`PoppieEulerOracle.sol:104`), and the cumulative check uses the same bps maths (`:297`). A −50% push passes a 5000 bps threshold, the single-stock default in `script/ConfigureDev.s.sol:27`.
- Thresholds are capped at 10000 bps (`:162-163`), measured against the old price (`:306-311`). Moves above +100% (reverse split on a per-token price, squeezes, leveraged ETFs) need `adminSetPrice`.
- Tests: `test_split2to1_mispush_passes_afterRotation`, `test_split2to1_mispush_passes_withinWindow`, `test_split3to1_needsAdmin`, `test_reverseSplit1to3_needsAdminEvenAtMaxThresholds`.
- Pricing the wrapper share as equity price × equity per share keeps the on-chain value continuous when the multiplier activates (`test_adapterPricesWrapperShareThroughSplit`, `test_B7_path1_wrapperShareIsBase_splitIsContinuous`). It only stays right if the keeper uses the multiplier in effect at the push block: xStocks multiplier activation (00:30 UTC) is not aligned with when split-adjusted prices start trading. The keeper can read the schedule on-chain through `multiplierUpdates`, as Euler's own xStocks adapter does.
- Robinhood (static balances, token as base) gives the same continuity without a wrapper. A keeper that ignores the multiplier pushes −75% at a 4:1 split (rejected by a 5000 bps breaker), −50% at 2:1 (passes 5000, rejected at 4000), and +900% at a 1:10 reverse split, which no threshold allows (`test_RH_mispush4to1_rejectedByBreaker`, `test_RH_mispush2to1_passes5000_rejectedBelow`, `test_RH_mispushReverseSplit_neverPasses`). The correct push at any ratio is 0%, so Robinhood reverse splits need no `adminSetPrice`.
- A keeper that follows an unexplained multiplier change (RH-6) moves collateral value with it, within the breaker: a ×1.4 change passes a 5000 bps breaker (`test_RH_rogueMultiplierDoesNotMoveValue`). The keeper must only apply multiplier changes that match a published corporate action.
- Wrapping an issuer token in an unmodified OZ `ERC4626` is workable: splits, reverse splits and reinvested dividends are shared pro rata and everyone can exit in full (`test_rebasesAreProRata`); with a 1e18 seed a donation attack costs the attacker the donation and the victim loses only rounding (`test_inflationAttackWithSeed`); and if the issuer token rounds transfers down, each deposit leaks a few wei from existing holders but never causes insolvency (`test_transferDriftIsBounded`, under a modelled rounding rule).
